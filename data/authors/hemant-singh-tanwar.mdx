Dr. Hemant Singh Tanwar is an Assistant Professor at the School of Internal Security, Defence and Strategic Studies (SISDSS) at Rashtriya Raksha University, an Institute of National Importance under the Ministry of Home Affairs, Government of India. He holds a Ph.D., M.Phil., and M.A. in Defence and Strategic Studies from Panjab University, Chandigarh. His academic and professional work focuses on defence studies, internal security, and strategic issues critical to national security.
