Prakash Jangid is a Senior Research Fellow in the Department of Political Science, University of Rajasthan, India. His doctoral research presents a rigorous comparative analysis of the hybrid warfare strategies of Russia, Iran, and Pakistan. Previously, he served as a Researcher and Editorial Assistant with the Consortium of Indo-Pacific Researchers and the Journal of Indo-Pacific Affairs, Air University Press, USA. His research has been published on leading national and international platforms, including the London School of Economics South Asia Blog, The Diplomat, and the Stimson Center’s South Asian Voices. Mr. Jangid’s research interests include counter-terrorism, security studies, kinetic and non-kinetic warfare, hybrid and grey-zone conflicts, and the strategic doctrines of Russia, Iran, and Pakistan.
