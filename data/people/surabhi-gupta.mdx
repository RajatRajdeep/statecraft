Dr. Surabhi Gupta is Assistant Professor in Department of International Affairs and Security Studies(DIASS), Sardar Patel University of Police, Security and Criminal Justice, Jodhpur, Rajasthan, India. She is also Deputy Director, Centre of Excellence on United Nations Studies at Sardar Patel University of Police, Security and Criminal Justice, Jodhpur. She has completed her Ph.D. from the Centre for South Asian Studies, Jawaharlal Nehru University. Her research areas include South Asian politics, India's neighbourhood policy, international relations and security studies. Dr. Gupta has contributed many research articles in leading journals and chapters in edited volumes. She has also edited a book on "India and United Nations in the Changing World Order".
