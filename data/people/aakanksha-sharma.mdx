Aakanksha Sharma is a doctoral researcher in the Department of Political Science and Public Administration at Banasthali Vidyapith, Rajasthan, India. Her core academic and policy research focuses on India’s foreign policy orientation, Indo-Pacific geopolitics, and contemporary dynamics in international relations. In addition to her doctoral pursuits, she is a Fellow at the Forum for Global Studies, a prominent think tank where she contributes to mainstream strategic discourse. As an active commentator on international affairs, her writing frequently explores the intersection of global governance, energy security, and shifting geopolitical landscapes.
