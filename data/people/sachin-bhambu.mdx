Sachin Bhambu is a PhD Scholar in Political Science at Malaviya National Institute of Technology Jaipur, with research interests in South Asian regional cooperation, foreign policy, refugees, migration, and international politics. He holds an M.Phil. in South Asian Studies from University of Rajasthan and a Master’s degree in Political Science from University of Delhi. He has also served as Guest Faculty at the University of Delhi. His academic work has been presented at several national and international conferences, where he has received recognition including Best Paper Presenter awards. He has contributed research articles and analytical commentaries on diplomacy, governance, refugee issues, and international affairs, and is actively engaged in peer-review activities for reputed academic journals. His work combines policy analysis with grounded field research, particularly focusing on South Asian geopolitical and humanitarian concerns.
