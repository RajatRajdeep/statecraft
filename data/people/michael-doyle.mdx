Michael W. Doyle is a University Professor of International Affairs, Law and Political Science at Columbia University, a former assistant secretary-general of the United Nations and the author of _[Cold Peace: Avoiding the New Cold War](https://wwnorton.com/books/9781631496066)_ (WW Norton, 2023).
