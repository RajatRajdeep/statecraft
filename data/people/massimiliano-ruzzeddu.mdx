Dr. Massimiliano Ruzzeddu is Associate Professor in Sociology at University Niccolò Cusano, Rome, Italy. Fluent in Italian, English and French, his research focuses on complexity theory, innovation, social change, migration and political culture. He has authored numerous scholarly publications including books, research articles and international conference papers and holds leadership roles in academic organizations. He is the co-editor of the latest book, _[Society of the Future: A Social Science View of What We Assume and What We Can Expect](https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-3-032-11658-1)_, published by Springer Nature in 2026.
