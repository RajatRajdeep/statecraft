Danny Steed, PhD - Clinical Associate Professor in Cybersecurity - is an author, consultant, and public speaker. As an academic, Danny specialises in the fields of war studies, strategic studies, intelligence studies, and cybersecurity. He is author of the book [British Intelligence and Strategy in the Suez Crisis](https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-3-319-31453-2), (Routledge, 2016). His most recent book is titled [The Politics and Technology of Cyberspace](https://www.amazon.in/Politics-Technology-Cyberspace-Danny-Steed/dp/0367787997/ref=tmm_pap_swatch_0), released in paperback by Routledge in 2021. His forthcoming book, _British Intelligence, Security, and Cyber_, will be published by Bloomsbury in 2027.

A scholar-practitioner, Danny previously worked inside The Cabinet Office (UK) in operational cybersecurity. As a consultant he has worked with a wide range of industries advising and delivering cybersecurity guidance, training, and assessment.
