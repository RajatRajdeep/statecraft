Dr. Bashir Safi is a national security scholar and former advisor to the U.S. military in Afghanistan. He holds a Ph.D. in National Security Policy from Liberty University in Virginia. He mostly writes on irregular warfare, COIN, military doctrine, and the political and strategic factors that influence conflict’s operational outcomes.
