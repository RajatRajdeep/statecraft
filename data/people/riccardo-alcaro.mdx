Dr. Riccardo Alcaro is Research Coordinator and Head of the Global Actors programme at the Istituto Affari Internazionali (IAI). His main area of expertise is transatlantic relations, with a particular focus on US and European policies towards Europe’s surrounding regions. He has been a visiting fellow at the Brookings Institution in Washington and a fellow of the EU-wide programme European Foreign and Security Policy Studies (EFSPS). He has coordinated the EU-funded [TRANSWORLD](https://www.iai.it/en/research/transworld-redefining-transatlantic-relationship-and-its-role-shaping-global-governance) project on transatlantic relations and global governance (7th Framework Programme) and the JOINT project on EU foreign and security policy (Horizon 2020). Riccardo is the author of _[Europe and Iran’s Nuclear Crisis](https://www.iai.it/en/publications/c09/europe-and-irans-nuclear-crisis-lead-groups-and-eu-foreign-policy-making)_ (Palgrave Macmillan, 2018) and co-author, with Pol Bargués, of _[Conflict Management and the Future of EU Foreign and Security Policy: Relational Power Europe](https://www.iai.it/en/publications/c01/conflict-management-and-future-eu-foreign-and-security-policy)_ (Routledge, 2025). He also edited _[The Liberal Order and its Contestations]( https://www.iai.it/en/publications/c08/liberal-order-and-its-contestations)_ (Routledge, 2018). He holds a summa cum laude PhD from the University of Tübingen.
