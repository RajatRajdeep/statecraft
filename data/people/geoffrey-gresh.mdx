Dr. Geoffrey F. Gresh is Professor of International Security Studies at National Defense University in Washington, D.C., and the author of _[Leaning Seaward: Japan as a Maritime Great Power](https://yalebooks.yale.edu/book/9780300279757/leaning-seaward/)_ and _[To Rule Eurasia’s Waves](https://yalebooks.yale.edu/book/9780300234848/to-rule-eurasias-waves/)_.  He is also the Series Editor of [Palgrave Studies in Maritime Politics and Security](https://link.springer.com/series/16675) and the Editor-in-Chief of the [Journal of Strategic Security](https://digitalcommons.usf.edu/jss/).
