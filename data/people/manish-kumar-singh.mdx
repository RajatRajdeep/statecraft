Manish Kumar Singh is the Assistant Director (Maritime Studies) at Rashtriya Raksha University. An advocate specialising in international maritime law, his research focuses on coastal security, Indo-Pacific affairs, and maritime cyber security.
He recently served as an Official Rapporteur for the 46th Antarctic Treaty Consultative Meeting in Kochi.
