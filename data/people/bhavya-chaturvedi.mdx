Bhavya Chaturvedi is a Senior Research Fellow in the Department of Political Science, University of Rajasthan, India. His research work examines the intersection of energy diplomacy and Indo-Pacific geopolitics, with a particular focus on India-China relations, renewable energy transitions, and strategic connectivity.
