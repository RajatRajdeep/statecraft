Dr. Kalyan Singhal is a Distinguished University Professor and the Doris E. and Robert V. McCurdy Chair at the Merrick School of Business, University of Baltimore. He is a 1967 mechanical engineering graduate of the Indian Institute of Technology, Bombay, in Mumbai, who served on the faculty of the Indian Institute of Management, Bangalore, in Bengaluru. He edits two top-ranked journals: [Production and Operations Management](https://journals.sagepub.com/home/PAO), which is on the _Financial Times_ list of the 50 best business journals, and [Management and Business Review](https://journals.sagepub.com/home/MAB), which _Forbes_ described as a rival to the _Harvard Business Review_.
