Jagdish Singh Rajpurohit is an Assistant Professor in the Department of College Education, Government of Rajasthan. His research explores the dynamics of Inclusive Democracy, with a special focus on the role of state and non-state actors in tribal development and empowerment. His doctoral thesis, Inclusive Democracy: Role of State and Non-State Actors in Tribal Development, examines how governance mechanisms, civil society, and indigenous communities interact to address marginalisation. His work bridges Comparative Politics, Governance, and International Relations, particularly in the areas of indigenous rights, global development discourses, and the impact of international frameworks on the social governance in India.
