Dr. Beena is Assistant Professor at the Centre for International Studies, MERI Group of Institutions, New Delhi. She holds a PhD in South Asian Studies from the School of International Studies, Jawaharlal Nehru University, New Delhi. She has completed two Postdoctoral fellowships, one with the University of Delhi and the second with Rashtriya Uchchatar Shiksha Abhiyan (RUSA), Utkal University, Odisha.
