Ritu Thakur is a independent researcher and writer specialising in South Asian geopolitics and strategic affairs. Currently pursuing her Master’s in International Relations and Area Studies (IRAS), her work examines global power shifts, regional security dynamics, and emerging geopolitical trends, with a sharp focus on India’s strategic interests.
