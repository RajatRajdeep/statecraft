Veronika Torosyan hold a PhD is a researcher at the Institute of Oriental Studies in Yerevan, Armenia. Her research interests include post-Soviet regional studies, Turkish and Russian studies, and regional integration processes within the Eurasian Economic Union (EAEU) and the Organisation of Turkic States (OTS). Her current research focuses on Azerbaijan’s positioning within the “Turkic world,” with particular attention to bilateral and multilateral relations among the member states of the Organisation of Turkic States.
