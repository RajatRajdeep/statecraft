Vinícius G. Rodrigues Vieira is Professor of Political Science at the Brazilian Institute of Education, Development, and Research (IDP). He also teaches Economics and International Relations (IR) at the Armando Alvares Penteado Foundation (FAAP) and the Getúlio Vargas Foundation (FGV), São Paulo, Brazil. Being a former Fung Global Fellow at Princeton University, he holds a DPhil in IR from Nuffield College, Oxford, and in 2024 published the book Shaping Nations and Markets: Identity Capital, Trade, and the Populist Rage (Routledge). Since 2023, he has been serving as the co-chair of the International Political Economy Research Committee of the International Political Science Association (IPSA).
