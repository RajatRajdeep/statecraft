Fathi Jama is a MOFA Taiwan Fellow 2026 and a visiting scholar at the International Taiwan Studies Center at National Taiwan Normal University (NTNU). A researcher specializing in the politics and geopolitics of the Horn of Africa and Somaliland, his work focuses on Taiwan–Somaliland relations, economic cooperation, and the strategic significance of the Port of Berbera. His analysis and commentary are featured across various academic and policy platforms.
