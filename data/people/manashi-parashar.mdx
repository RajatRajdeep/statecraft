Manashi Parashar is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Public Administration at Assam Don Bosco University, Guwahati, India, and a PhD Research Scholar in the Department of Political Science at Cotton University, Guwahati. Her doctoral research examines narco-insurgency, drug trafficking, and national security in the Indo–Myanmar borderlands, with a particular focus on Mon District of Nagaland. Her research interests include international relations, borderland studies, non-traditional security, insurgency, and India's Act East Policy. She has published in peer-reviewed journals on India's strategic interests, China, border security, and regional geopolitics.
