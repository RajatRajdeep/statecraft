Takuma Matsu is a Research Fellow at the Sasakawa Peace Foundation in Tokyo. His research focuses on Indo-Pacific security, maritime security, multilateral and minilateral security cooperation, and wargaming. His recent work examines the development of flexible and functional forms of security cooperation in the Indo-Pacific. He previously served in the Japan Ground Self-Defense Force and holds an MA in International Relations from Sophia University.
