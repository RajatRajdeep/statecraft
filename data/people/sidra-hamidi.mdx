Dr. Sidra Hamidi is an Assistant Professor at Trinity College in Hartford, CT in the United States. Her book, [After Fission: Recognition and Contestation in the Atomic Age]( https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/after-fission/F63C0DAADDFEA6554AD827A79C5D4E8D), was published by Cambridge University Press in 2026 and her work has appeared in the _European Journal of International Relations_, _International Affairs_, and _The Nonproliferation Review_.
