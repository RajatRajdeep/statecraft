Dr Shahram Akbarzadeh is Professor of Middle East & Central Asian Politics, and Director of Middle East Studies Forum at Deakin University. He is a prolific researcher and author of [Middle East Politics and International Relations: Crisis Zone](https://www.routledge.com/Middle-East-Politics-and-International-Relations-Crisis-Zone/Akbarzadeh/p/book/9781032052571) (Routledge, 2023), listed in top 20 books to read in International Relations.
