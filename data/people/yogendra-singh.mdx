Yogendra Singh is a policy and legislative analyst with over four years of hands-on experience in political consultancy, legislative support, and constituency management in India. He holds a Master’s degree in Political Science from Jawaharlal Nehru University (JNU), New Delhi. Specialising in electoral affairs, Yogendra brings deep expertise in analyzing contemporary South Asian elections. His extensive fieldwork, data-driven insights, and strategic contributions to political campaigns and give him a profound understanding of democratic politics in the region.
