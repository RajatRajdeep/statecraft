Mridul Singh Tanwar is an independent researcher. He holds a Master of Arts (M.A.) in Political Science from the University of Delhi and is UGC-NET qualified. With five years of teaching experience, he has served as an ad-hoc faculty member at various government colleges in Rajasthan. His academic interests encompass South Asian politics, strategic studies, and Indian foreign policy.
