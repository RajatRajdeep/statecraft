Pratap Singh is an Assistant Professor in the Department of College Education, Government of Rajasthan. Dr. Singh’s doctoral research focused on the India-China border dispute, with an emphasis on its historical, military, and diplomatic dimensions. His research has been published in peer-reviewed academic journals and edited volumes across the domains of national security, conflict studies, border disputes, and India's strategic environment.
