Charles E. Ziegler is Professor of Political Science at the University of Louisville, Director of the Grawemeyer Award for Ideas Improving World Order, and Executive Director of the Louisville Committee on Foreign Relations. A specialist on foreign policy and Eurasia, Ziegler has authored or edited six books and over 120 articles. The paperback version of his [Russia in the Pacific](https://global.oup.com/academic/product/russia-in-the-pacific-9780197865279?cc=in&lang=en&) will be released by Oxford University Press in November 2026. He is currently working on a study of everyday life in the Soviet Union.
