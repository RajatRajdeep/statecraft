Dr. Patryk Kugiel is an Assistant Professor in the Faculty of Political Science and International Studies at the University of Warsaw. His research focusing on Indian foreign policy includes major publications such as, "India’s Soft Power: A New Foreign Policy Strategy" (Routledge, 2017) and "India’s Policy Towards the European Union in the Post-Cold War Era: Partners in the Liberal Order?" (Routledge, 2025).
