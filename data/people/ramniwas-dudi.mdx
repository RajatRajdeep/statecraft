Ramniwas Dudi is an Advocate, practising commercial law, arbitration, mediation and litigation before the Rajasthan High Court and the District Court, Jodhpur, with experience in criminal law proceedings, legal research and drafting. He holds a graduation in Political Science (Honours) from Hindu College, and an LL.B. and LL.M. from Campus Law Centre, University of Delhi. His research interests lie in public international law, particularly the legal frameworks, mandates and accountability of UN peacekeeping operations, and the application of international legal norms in conflict-affected regions.
