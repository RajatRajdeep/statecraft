Ramniwas Dudi is an Advocate with around three years of practice across commercial law, arbitration, mediation, and litigation. He has practiced before the Rajasthan High Court and the District Court, Jodhpur, assisting in criminal law proceedings, legal research and drafting. Alongside his practice, his academic and research interests center on public international law – specifically the legal frameworks governing United Nations peacekeeping operations, the mandates and accountability of peace missions, and the application of international legal norms in conflict-affected regions.
