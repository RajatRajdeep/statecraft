Agnieszka Nitza-Makowska is a Research Fellow in Asian Studies at the Asia Centre, University of Tartu, Estonia; an Assistant Professor at the Institute of Political Science and International Relations at Civitas University in Warsaw, Poland; and an analyst at the Academic Centre for Strategic Analysis, War Studies University, Warsaw, Poland. 

Her scholarly interests encompass foreign policy, soft power, small states, and sustainable development, with a regional focus on China, India, Pakistan, and the Baltic states. She has published widely in peer-reviewed journals. Her most recent publications include “Non-Alignment 2.0 and South Asia’s Neutrality in the Russia–Ukraine War” (_Demokratizatsiya: The Journal of Post-Soviet Democratization_, 2026), “Is China Losing Its Soft Power in the Ukraine War? Evidence from the Indo-Pacific” (_Asia Europe Journal_, 2026), and the book chapter “Global South Agency in the International Climate Regime: China’s Status as a ‘Responsible Great Power’ in the Spotlight,” published in _India, China, and Climate Change Governance_ (Routledge, 2026). She is co-editor, with Amit Ranjan and Elo Süld, of _India, China, and Climate Change Governance: Leadership and Responsibility in the Asian Global South_ (Routledge, 2026). 

Agnieszka has served as a co-investigator on the projects _The Political, Economic and Cultural Role of Asia for Northern and Eastern Europe_ (2022–2023), _The Impact of the Ukrainian War on Geopolitical Developments in South Asia_ (2022–2023), and _China’s and India’s Foreign Policy and Sustainable Development in the New Geopolitical Context_ (since 2023). From 2021 to 2025, she led the Polish National Science Centre SONATA 16 project _Environmental Diplomacy as a Soft Power Instrument: China and the Belt and Road Initiative_. She was awarded a fellowship at the Center for Global Development, Shanghai University (2021–2022), and undertook research and teaching stays at Humboldt-Universität zu Berlin and Sciences Po in Paris. Beyond academia, she has provided expert analysis and policy advice to governmental bodies in EU member states.
