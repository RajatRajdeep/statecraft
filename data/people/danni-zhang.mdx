Danni Zhang is a joint PhD researcher at Northeastern University London and the University of Kent, affiliated with the Institute of Cyber Security for Society. Her research interests include global digital governance, digital geopolitics, and the international politics of data, and AI, with particular attention to the European Union, China, and competing visions of digital order. She is a 2026-2027 Northeastern University Humanities Center Global Fellow for a project on digital governance and changing architecture of sovereignty. Her work has appeared in _Politics and Governance and Journal of Current Chinese Affairs_.
