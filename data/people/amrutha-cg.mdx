Amrutha C.G. is a Doctoral Fellow in International Relations and Politics at the Central University of Kerala, Kasaragod. Her doctoral research focuses on India–Afghanistan relations in the post-U.S. era. Her broader academic interests include Strategic Studies, Soft Power Diplomacy, Peace and Conflict Studies, Migration, and South Asian Studies.
