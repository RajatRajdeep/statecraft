Dr. Garima Kumawat is a scholar-practitioner in the fields of maritime security, strategic studies, and international relations, with over a decade of academic and policy research experience spanning India's premier defence and security institutions. She currently serves as a Senior Fellow at the Centre for Joint Warfare Studies (CJWS), Headquarters Integrated Defence Staff, Ministry of Defence, Government of India. Previously, Dr. Kumawat served as Assistant Professor in the School of Internal Security, Defence and Strategic Studies (SISDSS) at Rashtriya Raksha University (RRU), Gandhinagar, Gujarat, an Institution of National Importance established under the Ministry of Home Affairs, Government of India. At RRU, she taught graduate and postgraduate courses in Maritime Security, Western and Oriental Strategic Thought, International Relations, and Theories of War and Peace, contributing to the intellectual formation of India's next generation of security professionals. Dr. Kumawat holds a PhD in Maritime Security from the Department of International Affairs and Security Studies, Sardar Patel University of Police, Security and Criminal Justice, Jodhpur. Her doctoral research was supported by a full-term Doctoral Fellowship awarded by the Indian Council for Social Science Research (ICSSR), Ministry of Education, Government of India. Beyond academia, she has engaged actively with the policy community, having served as Director of the Security and Foreign Policy Initiative at Jasol Dialogue, where she contributed to policy discourse on India's external security environment and regional strategic architecture. Her scholarly output includes research papers, analytical articles, and book chapter contributions published in Scopus-indexed journals and UGC-CARE listed journals, reflecting a commitment to peer-reviewed, high-quality academic publication. Dr. Kumawat's areas of research include India's Maritime Security, Indo-Pacific studies, India's national security and foreign Policy, world politics, defence, and strategic studies.
