Dr Emma Hutchison was Associate Professor in School of Political Science and International Studies (POLSIS) at The University of Queensland. Her work examined the politics of emotion, trauma, conflict, and humanitarianism and aid. Her book Affective Communities in World Politics (Cambridge University Press) was awarded the British International Studies Association Susan Strange Book Prize, the International Studies Association International Theory Best Book Award and the Australian Political Studies Association Crisp Prize.
