Kajol Gupta is a research scholar in the Department of Political Science at the University of Burdwan, West Bengal, India. Her research focuses on transboundary water governance, hydro-politics, and peace and conflict studies. Her work examines how water-sharing conflicts affect regional cooperation, environmental security, and the everyday lives of river-dependent communities.
