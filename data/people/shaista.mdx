Shaista is an Independent Researcher based in New Delhi, specialising in security studies and international relations. Previously, she served as a faculty member at the Tata Institute of Social Sciences (TISS), Maharashtra, and at the ICFAI University, Jaipur, where she taught courses on International Relations and Political Science. Her current research focuses on American military infrastructure in the Gulf, regional security dynamics in West Asia, and the complexities of the contemporary refugee crisis.
