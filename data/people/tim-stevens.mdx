Tim Stevens is Professor of International Security in the Department of War Studies, King's College London, UK, and founder of the KCL Cyber Security Research Group. He has published widely on cybersecurity, cyberwarfare and cyber policy, and is the Editor-in-Chief of Journal of Cyber Policy. His recent books include [Cyber Risk: Managing Uncertainty in a Digital World]( https://bristoluniversitypress.co.uk/beyond-cybersecurity) (Bristol University Press, 2026), [Research Handbook on Cyberwarfare]( https://www.e-elgar.com/shop/gbp/research-handbook-on-cyberwarfare-9781803924847.html?srsltid=AYCX9vKbE4As479URtfBc4GOdWgQ8EQglSZ_Sfgmd0ff7afqogXUpD9h) (Edward Elgar, 2024), and [What Is Cybersecurity For?]( https://bristoluniversitypress.co.uk/trade/what-is-cybersecurity-for) (Bristol University Press, 2023). Professor Stevens holds visiting appointments at Cnam (Paris) and UNED (Madrid), and is a frequent commentator on cyber issues for national and international media.
