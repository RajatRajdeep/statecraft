Thomas Crosbie is an Associate Professor at the Royal Danish Defence College and a NATO-qualified operational-level planner. He holds a Ph.D. in sociology from Yale University and is the author of _[The Political Army: How the U.S. Military Learned to Manage the Media and Public Opinion](https://cup.columbia.edu/book/the-political-army/9780231219785/)_ (Columbia University Press 2025), which was awarded the Giuseppe Caforio ERGOMAS Best Book Award.
